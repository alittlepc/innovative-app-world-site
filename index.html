The IAW Routing Protocol

The IAW Rail is a high-fidelity programmable money interface designed for the XRPL ecosystem. Its primary purpose is to simplify the orchestration of complex institutional outflows—such as tax reserves, payroll, and corporate treasury management—into a visual, "drag-and-drop" rail system. By utilizing the RLUSD stablecoin, every transaction is backed by 1:1 value parity with the US Dollar, providing the stability required for enterprise-grade financial operations.

Globally, the IAW Rail operates within the parameters of major regulatory frameworks including FATF, MiCA, and Basel III. The tool allows entities to build their own bespoke routing logic in seconds, ensuring that every dollar moved is automatically partitioned and sent to the correct compliance-ready vault or target account. This reduces human error in institutional distribution while maintaining the real-time speed of the XRP Ledger.
